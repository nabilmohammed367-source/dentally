## Answers to likely questions

- **Can we use it with our patients now?** No. It needs hosted operation, practice-level provisioning, reviewed data flows and agreements, phone/messaging delivery, and a safe connection or staff handoff to your practice software.
- **Does it book in our existing calendar?** No. It currently books in DentalOS's test schedule. We first need to learn which practice system you use and agree on the integration or staff handoff.
- **Will it call patients or send WhatsApp messages?** No provider is connected yet. The dashboard prepares and saves test records; it does not deliver them.
- **Is the voice receptionist ready?** The voice code exists and local guardrail tests pass, but a spoken test and inbound phone setup remain.
- **What does a pilot look like?** Start with one practice, one administrative workflow, synthetic rehearsal, documented staff review, and clearly agreed success measures. Only move to real patient data after the operational and privacy work is complete.
- **What is the price?** Do not imply a quote covers six fully deployed agents. Ask about call volume, message volume, current software, and the first workflow they want; then price the actual scope, setup, usage, and support in a written proposal.
